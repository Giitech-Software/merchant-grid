# Initial accounting posting rules

| Source event | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Posted credit/cash sale | Accounts Receivable or cash | Sales Revenue |
| Sale cost recognition | Cost of Goods Sold | Inventory |
| Customer collection | Cash/bank/mobile-money account | Accounts Receivable |
| Supplier payment | Accounts Payable | Cash/bank/mobile-money account |
| Expense payment | Expense account | Cash/bank/mobile-money account |

Each source transaction must post through the balanced journal service. Corrections use reversal and replacement entries. Account mapping is tenant-configurable and must be reviewed for local accounting and tax requirements.
