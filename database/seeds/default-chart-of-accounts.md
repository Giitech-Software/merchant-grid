# Default BuildMart chart of accounts

The initial account seed should create these tenant-scoped accounts:

- 1000 Cash
- 1010 Bank
- 1100 Accounts Receivable
- 1200 Inventory
- 2000 Accounts Payable
- 3000 Owner Equity
- 4000 Sales Revenue
- 5000 Cost of Goods Sold
- 6000 Operating Expenses

Account codes remain configurable per tenant; these are starter defaults, not jurisdiction-specific accounting advice.
